What it is

Your smart home, one glance away

The Home Assistant dashboard is great for full control — but for the things you check all day, opening a browser tab is detour. HA DeskLink flips it around: the information lives on your desktop, always visible, one keystroke away.

📊

Live sensor data

Temperatures, energy, motion — values from your instance right in the taskbar or as desktop widget.

💻

PC sensors into Home Assistant

CPU load, RAM, disk and uptime of your computer appear as entities in your instance.

🎛️

Entity controls

Toggle lights, switches and scenes straight from the tray menu without opening a browser.

🔔

Toast notifications

Get native desktop notifications when automations or events fire — with position and monitor choice.

🖥️

Embedded dashboard

Optional WebView view of your full dashboard, embedded natively in the app.

⚙️

Zero maintenance

Auto-update, autostart, dark & light theme, hotkeys. Set it up once, forget it's there.

How it compares

Ways to get your smart home on the PC

Browser / PWA

Full dashboard, but it's still just a browser window competing with your other tabs.

HASS.Agent

Feature-rich Windows integration with sensors and commands — great, but Windows-only.

HA DeskLink

Lightweight companion for all three desktop systems: live desktop view, notifications, PC sensors and an embedded dashboard — without running a browser.
