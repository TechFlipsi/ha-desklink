What is HA DeskLink?

HA DeskLink is a free, open-source desktop companion for Home Assistant. It connects directly to your Home Assistant instance and puts the information you care about right on your desktop — always visible, always one glance away.

While the official Home Assistant web dashboard is great for full control, HA DeskLink is built for the always-on view: a lightweight native app that lives in your taskbar or system tray.

Features

  • Live sensor data from Home Assistant on your desktop
  • System status & PC sensors pushed into Home Assistant
  • Control entities directly from your desktop
  • Toast notifications for HA events & automations
  • WebView dashboard embedded — full Lovelace access
  • Dark & light theme, hotkeys, autostart
  • Auto-update, no browser, fully local connection

How it compares

Looking for ways to get Home Assistant on your PC? Common options and where HA DeskLink fits:

  • Browser tab / PWA — full dashboard, but just a browser window
  • HASS.Agent — powerful Windows integration with sensors & commands
  • HA DeskLink — lightweight companion: live desktop view, notifications, system sensors, WebView dashboard — for Windows, Linux and macOS
